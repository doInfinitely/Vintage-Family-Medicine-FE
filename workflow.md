# Main Website Workflow

The website will be the public front door for Vintage Family Medicine and Pediatrics.

Patients visit the website and choose what they need:

## 1. New Appointment

Patient clicks **Schedule Appointment**  
→ Website opens **Klara**  
→ Patient submits appointment request  
→ Clinic staff reviews the request in **Klara**  
→ Staff confirms visit type/provider  
→ Appointment is added or confirmed in **Athena**

## 2. Existing Patient Portal

Patient clicks **Patient Portal**  
→ Website opens **Athena**  
→ Patient logs in through Athena  
→ Patient can access records, appointments, billing, and portal tools

## 3. Pay Bill

Patient clicks **Pay Bill**  
→ Website opens **Athena**  
→ Patient pays through Athena

## 4. Message or Text the Clinic

Patient clicks **Message Us** or **Text Us**  
→ Website opens **Klara**  
→ Patient sends secure message  
→ Clinic staff responds in Klara

## 5. Call the Clinic

Patient clicks **Call Us**  
→ Patient’s phone calls the clinic number  
→ Call is handled through **RingCentral**

## 6. Video Visit

Patient clicks **Video Visit**  
→ Website explains video visit process  
→ Patient schedules/requests through **Klara** or **Athena**  
→ Clinic sends final video visit instructions

# Simple System Roles

- **Website**: Shows clinic information and sends patients to the right tool.
- **Athena**: Patient portal, billing, records, existing patient access, final appointment schedule.
- **Klara**: Appointment requests, secure messages, text, forms, video visit communication.
- **RingCentral**: Phone calls, voicemail, call routing, fax if needed.

# Important Rule

Patients do not log in to the website.

They log in through **Athena** for portal access.

The website does not store patient records, appointment requests, messages, payments, or medical information.
